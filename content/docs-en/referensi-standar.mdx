## 1. National Competency Standards & Official Policy

- **SKKNI No. 299 of 2020**: Indonesia’s National Work Competency Standard for the Information and Communication category (computer programming, consulting, and related activities) — Artificial Intelligence, Data Science subfield.
  - Official link: [skkni.kemnaker.go.id](https://skkni.kemnaker.go.id/tentang-skkni/dokumen)
- **National Artificial Intelligence Strategy (Stranas KA) 2020–2045**: The blueprint for developing Indonesia’s AI ecosystem toward Golden Indonesia.
- **Indonesia One Data Portal**: The recommended national open data repository for the capstone project.
  - Link: [data.go.id](https://data.go.id/)

## 2. Organizer Program & Academic Partners

- **Thematic Academy Digital Talent Scholarship (DTS)**: An intensive training scholarship program by the Human Resources Research and Development Agency of the Ministry of Communication and Informatics of the Republic of Indonesia.
  - Learning portal: [lms.kominfo.go.id](https://lms.kominfo.go.id/)
  - Official site: [digitalent.kominfo.go.id](https://digitalent.kominfo.go.id/)
- **ITB Artificial Intelligence Center & STEI ITB**:
  - School of Electrical Engineering and Informatics, Institut Teknologi Bandung.
  - Instructor coordinator email: `aicenter@itb.ac.id` / `windy@staff.stei.itb.ac.id`

## 3. Primary Academic Reference Books

1. **Russell, S., & Norvig, P.** (2020). *Artificial Intelligence: A Modern Approach* (4th Edition). Pearson.
2. **Géron, A.** (2022). *Hands-On Machine Learning with Scikit-Learn, Keras, and TensorFlow* (3rd Edition). O'Reilly Media.
3. **McKinney, W.** (2022). *Python for Data Analysis: Data Wrangling with pandas, NumPy, and Jupyter* (3rd Edition). O'Reilly Media.
4. **Purwarianti, A., Riyanto, B., Lestari, D. P., et al.** (2021). *Artificial Intelligence di Masa Pandemi*. ITB Press.
