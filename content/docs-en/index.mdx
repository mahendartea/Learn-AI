Welcome to the **Data Science & AI Knowledge Base**, curated and developed by **Mahendar Dwi Payana**.

This portal provides an in-depth learning guide, a structured syllabus, and hands-on modules to master the competencies of a **Data Scientist / Associate Data Scientist**. All materials are organized systematically around industry standards and national work competencies.

---

## Owner & Developer Profile

| Attribute | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Owner & Author** | **Mahendar Dwi Payana** |
| **Areas of Expertise** | Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Data Analytics & MLOps |
| **Primary Standard** | Indonesia’s National Work Competency Standard (**SKKNI No. 299 of 2020**), *Data Science* subfield |
| **Curriculum Structure** | 8 Weeks of Learning (16 Structured Thematic Sessions) |
| **Learning Approach** | Conceptual Theory, Mathematical Derivations, and Hands-On Python (70% Practice : 30% Theory) |

## Official Source Attribution

This portal is developed based on the official curriculum:
- **Original Program**: Thematic Academy Digital Talent Scholarship (TA-DTS 2021), Ministry of Communication and Informatics of the Republic of Indonesia (Kominfo RI).
- **Academic Partner**: School of Electrical Engineering and Informatics, **Institut Teknologi Bandung (STEI ITB)**.
- **Reference Instructor & Author**: **Ir. Windy Gambetta, MBA** *(windy@staff.stei.itb.ac.id)* and the ITB AI Center team.
- **Competency Regulation**: Ministry of Manpower Decree No. 299 of 2020 on the SKKNI for Artificial Intelligence, Data Science subfield.
