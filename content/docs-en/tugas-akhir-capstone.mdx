## 1. Main Objective

The **Capstone Project** is the proof of integration for all 16 learning outcomes mastered during the 8-week program.

Participants are challenged to apply the **End-to-End Data Science Lifecycle** to solve real problems faced by institutions, universities, government agencies, or industry.

---

## 2. Team & Governance Requirements

- **Group Formation**: Each group consists of **3 members** (or 4 members for one group if the participant count is odd).
- **Suggested Team Roles**:
  - **Data / ML Engineer**: Responsible for data acquisition, pre-processing, scaling pipeline, and API deployment.
  - **Data Scientist / Modeler**: Responsible for problem formulation, EDA, algorithm experiments, and model tuning validation.
  - **Full-Stack / Product Lead**: Responsible for web interface design, visualization integration, and the business evaluation report.

---

## 3. Dataset Selection Ethics (Critical)

The committee and curriculum enforce strict rules on data integrity:

### A. Allowed & Recommended Data
1. **Own Organizational Real Data**: Internal data from universities, research labs, employers, or business partners.
2. **National Open Data**: Official data from Indonesian government portals, such as:
   - **Indonesia One Data Portal**: [data.go.id](https://data.go.id/)
   - **Provincial/City Open Data**: e.g., [data.jakarta.go.id](https://data.jakarta.go.id/), [opendata.jabarprov.go.id](https://opendata.jabarprov.go.id/)
3. **Ethics & Anonymization**: Any data containing confidential personal identity (*Personally Identifiable Information - PII*) must be masked or encrypted before use.

### B. Strict Prohibitions (Automatic Disqualification)
> [!CAUTION]
> It is strictly forbidden to use clean, popular *toy datasets* commonly found in online tutorials, such as:
> - General Kaggle Datasets (Titanic, Iris, House Prices, generic Credit Card Fraud).
> - The UCI Machine Learning Repository (`archive.ics.uci.edu`).
> - Old academic benchmark datasets (plain MNIST, CIFAR-10, Boston Housing).

## 5. Final Assessment Criteria (50% of Passing Grade)

1. **Business Relevance (15%)**: Sharpness in mapping a real business problem into a measurable machine learning formulation.
2. **Data Preparation & EDA Rigor (25%)**: Data cleanliness, rational imputation, creative feature engineering, and freedom from data leakage.
3. **Modeling & Validation Rigor (25%)**: Appropriate evaluation metrics, Stratified Cross-Validation scenarios, and fair algorithm comparison.
4. **Operational Web App Quality (25%)**: Ease of use for non-technical users, stable prediction inference, and cloud response time.
5. **Documentation & Final Report (10%)**: Clarity of the GitHub repository structure, README, and scientific conclusions.
