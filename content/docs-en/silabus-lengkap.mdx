## 16-Session Curriculum Matrix

| Session | Topic | Learning Outcome | SKKNI Unit Code | Hours |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- | :---: |
| **01** | **Modern Computing Environment Setup & AI Tools** | Prepare a Python 3.10+ interpreter, virtual environments (venv/conda), JupyterLab, VS Code, Google Colab, and GPU acceleration (CUDA/MPS). | Computing Foundations | 2 JP Class<br />4 JP Self-study |
| **02** | **AI Fundamentals, Technology & Applications** | Understand the 4 AI definition paradigms, PEAS intelligent agent architecture, 7 task-environment types, problem-solving taxonomy, Industry 4.0 ecosystem, and Stranas KA 2020–2045. | AI Foundations | 2 JP Class<br />4 JP Self-study |
| **03** | **Data-Driven Data Science Methodology** | Master industry methodologies (CRISP-DM, KDD, SEMMA, TDSP), 21 SKKNI No. 299/2020 units, and an integrated Python case study on DKI 2016 infant immunization data. | AI Methodology | 2 JP Class<br />4 JP Self-study |
| **04** | **Tools for Data Science** | Master Python advantages, IDE/Colab ecosystem, Git/GitHub, 6 core libraries, NumPy vectorization vs. list TypeError, and analysis of `Tab.csv` and `pmp_2020_baru.csv`. | Numeric Computing | 2 JP Class<br />4 JP Self-study |
| **05** | **Business Understanding & AI Project Planning** | Formulate AI vs. automation, end-to-end ML paradigms, asymmetric financial cost analysis, and real AI project design (Tata Global stock & autonomous vehicles). | `J.62DMI00.001.1`<br />`J.62DMI00.002.1`<br />`J.62DMI00.003.1` | 2 JP Class<br />4 JP Self-study |
| **06** | **Data Understanding (Non-Visualization)** | Master Stevens’ 4 scales, 3 structured data models, multi-source data acquisition techniques, non-visual descriptive statistics, and case studies on car characteristics and EPL players. | `J.62DMI00.004.1`<br />`J.62DMI00.005.1` | 2 JP Class<br />4 JP Self-study |
| **07** | **Exploratory Data Analysis & Statistical Visualization** | Master variable vs. statistical visualization, one-way ANOVA F-test (car wheel drive), and complete 5 official statistics notebook exercises. | `J.62DMI00.005.1` | 2 JP Class<br />4 JP Self-study |
| **08** | **Data Preparation: Object Selection, Cleaning & Validation** | Determine data objects via sampling (probability vs. non-probability), handle imbalanced datasets (SMOTE), 3 feature selection techniques (Chi2, Extra Trees, Heatmap), and clean real datasets (British Library, University Towns, Olympics). | `J.62DMI00.006.1`<br />`J.62DMI00.007.1`<br />`J.62DMI00.008.1` | 2 JP Class<br />4 JP Self-study |
| **09** | **Numeric Data Construction & Transformation** | Perform normalization, standardization (MinMax, Robust, Standard Scaler), and distribution transformation (Log, Box-Cox, Yeo-Johnson). | `J.62DMI00.009.1` | 2 JP Class<br />4 JP Self-study |
| **10** | **Feature Engineering, Encoding, Data Integration & Labeling** | Perform categorical encoding (One-Hot, Dummy, Target), table integration (Merge & Join), PCA feature extraction, Pseudo-Labelling, feature selection, and label assignment. | `J.62DMI00.009.1`<br />`J.62DMI00.010.1`<br />`J.62DMI00.011.1` | 2 JP Class<br />4 JP Self-study |
| **11** | **Modeling Scenarios & Supervised Classification** | Design validation scenarios (Train-Validation Split, K-Fold, Scikit-learn Pipeline) and train/tune classification models (Logistic Regression, SVM, Decision Tree, Naive Bayes, KNN, AdaBoost) on the Iris dataset. | `J.62DMI00.012.1`<br />`J.62DMI00.013.1` | 2 JP Class<br />4 JP Self-study |
| **12** | **Numeric Regression Modeling** | Train simple & multiple linear regression, polynomial/non-linear regression, SVR, Decision Tree, and Random Forest Regression; test Gauss-Markov assumptions and evaluate with MAE/MSE/RMSE/R². | `J.62DMI00.013.1` | 2 JP Class<br />4 JP Self-study |
| **13** | **Unsupervised Learning & Clustering** | Perform unsupervised data segmentation using K-Means++, Hierarchical Agglomerative, DBSCAN, and Silhouette evaluation. | `J.62DMI00.013.1` | 2 JP Class<br />4 JP Self-study |
| **14** | **Deep Learning & Artificial Neural Networks** | Understand Perceptron, Multilayer Perceptron, activation functions, Backpropagation, gradient descent, adaptive learning rate (Adam), and apply ANN, CNN (Conv/Pooling), and RNN with Scikit-learn & Keras. | `J.62DMI00.013.1` | 2 JP Class<br />4 JP Self-study |
| **15** | **Model Evaluation & Diagnostic Review** | Evaluate model performance via Confusion Matrix, Precision/Recall/F1, ROC-AUC, PR-Curve, Log Loss, regression metrics (MAE/MSE/RMSE/R²), clustering metrics (Silhouette/Davies-Bouldin), model comparison (McNemar’s Test), and feature interpretation with SHAP. | `J.62DMI00.014.1`<br />`J.62DMI00.015.1` | 2 JP Class<br />4 JP Self-study |
| **16** | **Web Model Deployment & MLOps** | Understand deployment concepts & patterns (static, dynamic on-device/on-server), Single/Silent/Canary strategies, reproducibility, model serialization (pickle/H5), and deploy Linear Regression & ANN models to the web (Flask/Heroku, FastAPI/Streamlit) with drift monitoring. | `J.62DMI00.016.1`<br />`J.62DMI00.017.1` | 2 JP Class<br />4 JP Self-study |

## Credit Hour Distribution

- **Total Sessions**: 16 Sessions (8 Weeks / Saturday & Sunday)
- **Synchronous Live Sessions**: 16 sessions × 2 JP = **32 JP**
- **Self-Paced Learning (Hands-on & LMS)**: 16 sessions × 4 JP = **64 JP**
- **Total Learning Load**: **96 Credit Hours (JP)** (@ 45 minutes/JP)
